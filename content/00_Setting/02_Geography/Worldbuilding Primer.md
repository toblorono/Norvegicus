### Quarlimne
**Location**: Marsh, Plains
**Myrk Density**: Low
**Population**: Medium-Large. Human (6), Elf (3), Dwarf (2)
**Points of Interest**: The King's Palace, The Academy 
**Reference**: Venice, London (Assassins Creed Syndicate)

The capitol of Midrealm is situated in the historic ruins of a world before myrk. Quarlimne is a marriage of ruin and restoration. Its withstanding architecture spreads across a marshland, and the city's pulse is serviced with a network of tangled canals. The royal family has ruled in Quarlimne since the emergence of myrk had destabilized the previous civilization. Through its naturally low concentration of myrk, along with artificial means of suppression, the safety of Quarlimne has fostered a growing and diverse population. Here, many come to seek knowledge by studying the past to step boldly into the future.
### Allium Trico
**Location**: Coastline (deep-bay)
**Myrk Density**: High
**Population**: Massive. 
**Points of Interest**: Levitram Depot, Majtech Headquarters
**Reference**: Piltover (Arcane), 

A.T., the Ramps, the Winding City. Allium Trico leverages its proximity to the bay for many of its water-based needs. However, the density of myrk pushes against the city's ability to sprawl, thus forcing its architecture to climb upwards. 

Here, the hub of levi-tram innovation keeps it connected with the other major cities of Midrealm. Innovation runs rampant along its disparate streets. The higher you are along its ramps, the harder it is for the myrk to seep through the mitigations. 

Where Quarlimne holds poise, the city of ramps rushes toward the future with reckless abandon. Businesses crack through innovations at an unprecedented rate, though not necessarily for the sake of altruism. Profit and discovery pulse through the city.
### Kremill Port (Cream City)
**Location**: Coastline (mid-bay)
**Myrk Density**: Medium-low
**Population**: Large. Human (4). Misc. Exotic (2)
**Points of Interest**: The Docks, Bazaar, Thieves' Guild
**Reference**: Slateport City (Pokemon),  

The City of Sails. Kremill Port serves as the region's preferred access to the Great Grand and the lands beyond its waters. Rare oddities are awaiting those willing to find them across the city's innumerable vendors. 
## Minor Cities
### City of Tunnels
At the eastern reaches of Midrealm is a place connected by tunnels. A vast web of stone joins its various buildings like a petrified root system. However, each of these tunnels are suspended in air, as if excavated from the earth itself.
### Venega
A quaint and quiet town praised for its idyllic slow pace. Seated to the east of the main levi-tram line between Kremill Port and Allium Trico.
### Corsair's Crossing
Across the Great Grand, Corsair's Crossing is the gateway to the untamed oceans of the southwest. Where Kremill is a city of an unspoken code, Corsair is entirely lawless.
