## Background
Eclipse is a piece of the shattered moon, fallen from Corjian's skies. Arython recovered and attuned this piece of the moon during his ecological studies.
## Manifestations
### Dormant
**Empowered**. Your Lightning Launcher and Thunder Gauntlets gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

**Overcharge**. As a bonus action, you can channel the power of the moon into your Arcane Armor for one minute. During this time, your Lightning Launcher and Thunder Gauntlets deal an extra 1d4 Force damage and you gain a +1 bonus to your AC. Once used, you cannot use this feature again until you finish a long rest.

### Ascended State
**Ascended Overcharge**. Your number of uses increases to 2 per long rest and the additional force damage increases to 2d4.

**Smort**. Your Intelligence score increases by 2 to a maximum of 22.

**Moon Fire**. When you hit a creature with your Lightning Launcher or Thunder Gauntlets, you can choose to empower the hit with moonlight. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving through against your spell save DC or be subjected to the effects of the Faerie Fire spell. You must maintain concentration on this effect as though you were concentrating on a spell. You can use this ability a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus and regain all uses when you complete a long rest.

**Deflective Force**. You can user your reaction and expend a use of Starlight Step to cast the Shield spell without consuming a spell slot or requiring material components. If you use this ability against a melee attack and the attack still hits, you take half damage from the attack and deflect the other half of the attack's damage back onto the attacker. Your use of Deflective Force in this way must be declared before the damage of the attack is announced.
### Exalted
**Exalted Overcharge**. Your number of uses increases to 3 per long rest, the bonus to your AC increases to +2, and the additional force damage increases to 3d4.

**Improved Empowerment**. The attack and damage bonus to your Lightning Launcher and Thunder Gauntlets increases to +2.,

**Telekinetic Master**. You gain the Psi Warrior's Telekinetic Master feature.

**Quick Change!** You can change your armor between Guardian and Infiltrator modes as an action.

You may increase one ability score of your choice by 2d4, up to a maximum of 30, for 24 hours. Once used, this feature cannot be used again for 100 years.
